Killing time was never this much fun! Play gruesome games with the creepy Twins, destroy the Dark Heart and escape the warden's temporal prison!

Key Features

This is a walkthrough of the key features of the map, such as Pack-a-Punch, Wonder Weapons, etc.

  1. Get 4 parts:
    • The Sealant: In Normal Nuketown, go to the bedroom in Yellow House Upstairs and get it from the bottom of the shelf near the window.
    • The Barrel: Find the Cyst that is emitting a humming sound in Destroyed Nuketown and feed it souls until it drops The Hammer.
    • The Hammer:
      1. Get the SO3 Vial found on the desk in Destroyed Yellow House Upstairs then interact with the kitchen sink in Destroyed Green House to get the H2SO4 Vial. The Vial can be used 3 times before the need to refill it at the sink.
      2. In Destroyed Nuketown, find the mannequin that emits a humming sound. There is a total of 12 mannequins; 9 around Cul-De-Sac and the houses and 3 in Trinity Ave.:
        • Behind the swing in Yellow House Backyard
        • Inside the garage of Green House, next to the Crafting Table.
        • Near the big garage door in Trinity Ave.
        • In Green House Backyard, behind the Perk location.
      3. Return to Normal Nuketown and pour the H2SO4 Vial on the corresponding mannequin.
      4. Go to Destroyed Nuketown, and if done correctly, the mannequin will have dissolved and dropped The Hammer.
    • The Stock:
      1. In Destroyed Nuketown, find black goo on a wall that is emitting a humming sound. There are multiple locations.
      2. Return to Normal Nuketown, find the corresponding wall and throw an explosive to the wall to create a hole.
      3. Go back to Destroyed Nuketown. If done correctly, the hole will have The Stock inside available to be picked up.
  2. Melee the workbench in Destroyed Truck Interior to open and build the Blundergat.

Sundergat Upgrade

  1. After a few rounds in Destroyed Nuketown, a Tortured Zombie will spawn surrounded by a fire tornado. Bring the Tortured Zombie next to the bench and kill it with the Blundergat. Note: if the Tortured Zombie is near other zombies, it will weaken and die after its health bar is depleted.
  2. Wait until the next round, then repeat the above step.
  3. In the next round, a third Tortured Zombie will spawn, but will transform into a Mimic when approaching. Repeat the process and kill it near the bench with the Blundergat.
  4. Travel back to Normal Nuketown and melee the workbench before placing the Blundergat.
  5. Return to Destroyed Nuketown, and melee the workbench again to pick up the Sundergat.

Main Quest

One-screen quick reference. Full details for every step are in the sections below.

① Setup

  1. Obtain the Blundergat and upgrade to the Sundergat.
  2. The Twins Swing: swing seat (Normal, Yellow House) → RC-XD race (Destroyed) → Chalk → rehang swing → Twins scene.

The next three sections can be done in any order / simultaneously.

② Strange Tree / Hopscotch

  1. Irradiated Seeds (Destroyed garage toolbox) → plant in Normal Trinity Ave mound → fill tree with souls (Blundergat/Sundergat).
  2. 3 Combat Axes → Strange Firewood → fireplace (Molotov).
  3. Stand on the massive X in Destroyed Trinity Ave until it stays blue → Normal: hopscotch scene → jump 1→12→1 (avoid smoke, 30s fail wait).
  4. Golden music box spawns → fill with souls (Sundergat) → escort to fireplace.

③ Piano / Bouncing Ball

  1. Destroyed Green House: turn Piano Teacher (Brain Rot/Psych) → teleport back → it plays piano & explodes.
  2. 8 glowing notes in Destroyed — blink order 1–8 → interact in order → piano order on wall is always 86756535.
  3. Kill 3 floating zombies → souls over the X → Normal: ball trial — kill airborne zombies before ball lands → music sheet → escort to piano (Sundergat).

④ Toy Box / Four Square

  1. Destroyed PaP area: shoot speaker pole → Goggles (use Wisp Tea to bring in).
  2. Death Perception: find Headset outline → Normal toy box scene.
  3. Red ball → shoot to the massive X in Destroyed Green House Backyard → Four Square trial — bounce ball square-to-square until timer ends.
  4. Cymbals spawn → fill with souls (Sundergat) → escort to toy box.

⑤ Clock Tower

  1. Destroyed: shoot crumbled clock → hands to 0.
  2. Teleport to Normal; shoot the hands again during the teleport animation.
  3. White orb appears → scene: piano → toy box → fireplace → Twins at the swing with a yellow portal → boss fight.

Phase 1

  1. The Dark Heart will spawn three massive pile of black goo around the map. One a Trinity Ave., one at Yellow House Backyard and one at Green House Backyard.
  2. Each pile of goo will have a cyst attached near it, kill zombies to fill each cyst with souls. If done correctly, the black goo will disappear revealing an object (Piano, Music Box, Fireplace)
  3. One of the three object will have a Concentration Field around it. Defend the field from zombies and enemies before they can destroy it. Once it is done, the field will move to the next object.
  4. Once all three objects are done, the Dark Heart will be damageable in glowing orange areas. The Heart will periodically conjure meteors as well as fire tornadoes. The tornadoes can be redirected by throwing Molotovs in their paths.
  5. Once a third of the Heart's health bar is depleted, it will go into immune state again, and a Max Ammo and Max Armor power-up will spawn in front of Green House.

Phase 2

  1. During this phase, Rad-Hounds will spawn in addition to regular zombies. 
  2. The objects are again covered with black goo with a green orb floating above them. Kill all the zombies that spawn from the green orb until the black goo disappears.
  3. Once again, a Concentration Field will appear around one object and move to the others once successfully defended. While defending, the Dark Heart may conjure meteors and fire tornadoes to attack the players. Repeat for all three objects to be able to damage the Dark Heart once more.
  4. Similar to last phase, once two thirds of the Heart's health bar is depleted, it will go into immune state again, and a Max Ammo and Max Armor power-up will spawn in front of Green House.

Phase 3

  1. During this phase, Shock Mimics will spawn in addition to regular zombies and Rad-Hounds.
  2. The objects are again covered with black goo. A Tortured Zombie will appear in the middle of the map, bring it to one of the objects and kill it to remove the goo. Note: Similar to the previous steps, the Tortured Zombie's health will gradually be drained when zombies are close by.
  3. Once again, a Concentration Field will appear around one object and move to the others once successfully defended. Similar to the second phase, repeat for all three objects to be able to damage the Dark Heart once more.
  4. Damage the Dark Heart until it is killed and the cutscene is triggered.

Relics

There are 3 Relics that can be unlocked from playing Paradox Junction, those Relics are then used as gameplay modifiers for Cursed mode.

The Relics are divided into three groups: Grim, Sinister, and Wicked. Each group fill the Cursed Tier bar differently with the Grim Relics filling up a third of the bar, the Sinister Relics filling up two thirds, and the Wicked Relics filling up the entire bar. As such you will need to apply different Relics to reach the desired Tier level.

  • Tier I: Any 3 Grim — Unlocks Golden Armor (45,000 Essence)
  • Tier II: 2 Sinister + 2 Grim (example loadout) — Unlocks Ultra Rarity (8,000 Salvage or 17,500 Salvage from default rarity)
  • Tier III: 3 Grim + 2 Sinister + 1 Wicked (example) — Unlocks Pack-a-Punch IV (100,000 Essence)

Make sure to scroll sideways to see the steps. Higher tiers just need the total difficulty value to match the target fill; swap relics as you discover them.

Grim Relics

ROCKET No Score Streaks
  1. Reach Round 20
  2. In Normal Nuketown, brain rot a Rad-Hound and pet it.
  3. Call in a DAWG Killstreak in Destroyed Nuketown, put it in Sentry Mode and pet it.
  4. Travel to Normal Nuketown and interact with the green portal in Green House Upstairs.
  5. Survive 4 waves while enemies only take damages from their elemental weakness. Shadow Rift for Toxic Zombies and Rad-Hounds
  6. Light Mend for Doppelghasts
  7. Brain Rot for Shock Mimics

Sinister Relics

SUMMONING KEY Zombies explode on death dealing damage to nearby players
  1. Throw a grenade inside the broken outside chimney of Destroyed Green House near Cul-De-Sac. This will drop the Dog Collar of Notso.
  2. Return to Normal Nuketown and turn a Rad-Hound using Brain Rot/Psych Grenade. If done correctly, the dog will start digging in Yellow House Backyard. The dog will then stop and you can pick up the ball in its mouth.
  3. Reach Round 40 and down yourself near the fences at the very back of Green House Backyard, behind the Perk location. If done correctly, a headstone will rise from the ground. Interact with the headstone to place the ball and dog collar.
  4. Return to Normal Nuketown and interact with the yellow portal against the wall of Yellow House Backyard.
  5. Survive 5 waves while killing all the enemies of each wave before the time runs out (90 seconds).

Wicked Relics

MANGLER HELMET No Arsenal
  1. Activate Cursed Mode Tier II. Do not pick the Seed Relic for this.
  2. Equip the Mister Peeks Field Upgrade and use the Mystery Box until Mister Peeks appears. Activate the Field Upgrade to drop Mister Peeks on the ground and pick it up. This needs to be done in both versions of Nuketown.
  3. Place Mister Peeks on the mailbox of Yellow House in both versions of Nuketown.
  4. Reach Round 60.
  5. Pick the Ultra Knife found in the mailbox of either version of Nuketown which will now be opened. Doing so will spawn a HVT Mimic. Kill the Mimic with the Knife, which can be Pack-a-Punched. Note: If you kill the Mimic with something else, it will eventually respawn the next round.
  6. If done correctly, travel to Destroyed Nuketown and interact with the red portal that spawned in front of the Green House front balcony.
  7. Survive 6 waves with all Armor Plates removed and with 50 less health point.
