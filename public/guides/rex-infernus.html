Rex Infernus | Complete Map Breakdown

As reality stands on the cusp of destruction, the crew face their ultimate challenge. Cleanse the Shadowsmith places of power, find the Warden's inner sanctum, and put an end to his loathsome existence.

Key Features

This is a walkthrough of the key features of the map, such as Pack-a-Punch, Wonder Weapons, etc.

  1. Activate the Pack-a-Punch Machine in Nexus Forge.
  2. Interact with one of the small pilars with a glowing green orb to collect the Void Claw.
    • Note: The Void Claw will only be available for about 30 seconds before it needs to be collected again.

Void Talon Upgrade

  1. From the Nexus Forge, look at Her House and shoot at the basketball located in the broken window. Shoot at it until it falls out of the map.
    • Note: This can be done immediately after stepping outside Her House before walking through portal.
  2. In the subsequent rounds, four blue symbols will appear on Her House.
    • Note: The symbols will appear one by one, one every round or two, make sure to note the order in which they appeared.
  3. On an Exfil round, shoot at the symbols in the order they appeared. If done correctly, the exfil booth will send you to Her House on completion. The Twins will appear and drop a Raw Aether Crystal.
  4. Inside Her House, travel upstair and shoot a charged shot of the Warden's Blight inside the bathroom. If done correctly, the Eye of the Forge will drop on the ground.
  5. Pick it up then wait for the portal to appear to return to Nexus Forge.
  6. Find one of the pillars with the glowing green orb and interact with it to empower it with the Eye of the Forge.
    • Note: With the Void Claw, it is possible to get the Eye of the Forge back into your inventory to empower another pedestal, such as the boss arena.
  7. If done correctly, the Void Talon will be available to pick up.
    • Note: The Void Talon lasts significantly longer than the Void Claw.

Main Quest

Note: Several of the items needed can be collected in any order, or prior to their respective steps. Likewise, steps can be done in any order or concurrently with others.

☁️ ① Swirling Pool

  1. Veytharion's Sanctuary: collect the 4 cubes (pillar by GobbleGum, 2 windows in Mule Kick room, near Void Claw pedestal).
  2. Left opening bottom→top: Fire, Hand, Water, Leaf. Shuffle to the right opening → Water, Leaf, Hand, Fire. Fire must never touch Leaf/Water; slide empties if a round ends.
  3. Shoot/melee the circle to move the slide. Reward: swirling pool + Random Perk. Interacting with the pool triggers rain the next round (needed for every temple cleansing).

⚙️ ② Nexus Forge

  1. With Void Talon: free the 3 pillar Pull Switches + 6 panels around the Nexus Core.
  2. Clear the webs (explosives/Molotov, Astral/Ursuper Flam, or Warden's Blight) — Deathspinners spawn.
  3. Purple orb → Pack-a-Punch → surge, then quickly interact the ground Pull Switch next to Deadshot Daiquiri at the Nexus Core bottom.
  4. Pillars around the Forge can now be moved.

🛕 ③ Cleanse the Temples

  1. Align the 3 pillars in front of each temple first.
  2. Per temple: quest item → cauldron + Astral Flame shot → attach floater to a zombie → its symbol matches a Titan Trap shackle (Caltheris / Veytharion Passage) → trap it.
  3. Tile mini-action → reflector: Dravakar re-plate several times · Nyxara prone · Caltheris aim 30 s · Veytharion keep jumping.
  4. Lift reflector (charged Warden's Blight) → aim beam at the marked wall → kill zombies beneath → wall breaks.
  5. Shoot Visage → lockdown → destroy 4 Shadow Souls → shoot again until screen turns white.
  6. Purple orb under the face + Warden's Blight lift → purple eyes → rain + Astral Flame to the face point = cleansed.
  • Items: Dravakar — Blacksmith's Hammer (Shatter Blast shields) · Nyxara — Ancient Scroll (Shadow Rift in forest) · Caltheris — Shimmering Thread (purple orbs) · Veytharion — Woven Sash (rain; Spira / Ruinas / Aranea Insula).

👹 ④ Warden Boss Fight

  1. Start in the Nexus Core circles. Void Claw/Talon recommended — he pushes you out of the arena.
  2. P1: Dread Skulls (Dead Wire) stun him → shoot the tail critical point.
  3. Immune: destroy Shadow Souls (Light Mend) → P2: destroy Stingers (Cryofreeze) or they trigger a wipe; dodge stun circles.
  4. Intermediate room: survive or kill the HVT. P3: armor-providing Shadow Souls + skulls. P4: more skulls before each stun.

  1. Travel to Veytharion's Sanctuary and acquire four cubes to bring into the room with the Void Claw pedestal. Note: Death Perception makes it easier to see them.

  2. Place the cubes on the wall in the left vertical opening in this order: Fire (bottom) - Hand - Water - Leaf (top).
  3. The goal is to move the cubes from the left vertical opening to the right vertical opening to form this order: Water (bottom) - Leaf - Hand - Fire (top)
    • Note: The Fire cube must never touch either the Leaf or Water cube, otherwise all the cubes will disappear and return to their original spots. Furthermore, if the cubes are not placed in a vertical opening when a round ends, they will return to their original spots.
  4. Transfer the cubes from the left opening to the right opening. Note: Shoot or melee the circle on the wall to move the slide back and forth between the two openings after placing the cubes in the slide.
    1. Transfer 1
      1. Put Hand and Fire cubes in the slide and bring it to the right.
      2. Place the Fire cube in the right opening.
      3. Bring back slide to the left.
    2. Transfer 2
      1. Add the Water cube in the slide and bring it to the right.
      2. Place the Hand cube in the right opening first, then place the Water cube. Put the Fire cube back in the slide first, then the Hand cube.
      3. Bring back slide to the left.
    3. Transfer 3
      1. Put Hand cube in the left opening first, then put the Fire cube.
      2. Put the Leaf cube in the slide, then the Hand cube.
      3. Place the Leaf cube in the right opening.
      4. Bring back the slide to the left.
    4. Transfer 4
      1. Put the Fire cube in the slide.
      2. Place the Hand cube in the right opening first, then the Fire cube.
  5. If done correctly, the wall will open revealing the swirling pool and a Random Perk drop.
    • Note: Interacting with the swirling pool will trigger rain the next round. This will be needed for the final step for each temple cleansing.

Travel to Nexus Core and stand in one of the circles to start the encounter. At the start of the fight, the Warden will take the form of a Scorpion-man. The Warden will move extremely fast, with the goal of pushing you out of the arena. The use of Void Claw (or Void Talon) is recommended.

Phase 1 - The Warden is accompanied by a Dread Skull (weak to Dead Wire), destroying the Dread Skull will stun the Warden revealing a critical point at the end of its tail. Repeat the process until a third of the health is gone and the Warden becomes immune. While it is immune, Shadow Soul (weak to Light Mend) will appear around the map, destroy them all to start Phase 2.

Phase 2 - In addition to the Dread Skulls, the Warden will spawn floating circles that will stun and deal damages. Moreover, Warden's Stingers (weak to Cryofreeze) will appear around the arena. It is important to destroy as many stingers otherwise they can create a wipe attack. Continue until only a third of its health remains.

Intermediate Phase - The Warden will send you to a close room where the objective will be to either survive a specific amount of time, or to eliminate a HVT. Once the objective is complete, you will be teleported back into the arena.

Phase 3 - Alongside the Warden's Stingers, circles, Dread Skulls, Shadow Souls will appear around the arena providing armor to the Warden and need to be destroyed. Repeat the same process until there is no more health.

Phase 4 - A surprise fourth phase will start. The Warden will have a third of health remaining and even more Shadow Souls providing armor. Additionally, several Dread Skulls need to be destroyed before the Warden can be stunned. Repeat the same process until the fight is finally done.

Side Quests and Easter Eggs

Find three Mr Peeks headphones around the map:

  • On top of a pillar in Nyxara Main Chamber, requires wall-jumping to reach.

  • At a window in Ruinas Insula.

  • Around a skull hidden between a rock and a low wall in Arena Insula.

"All We Are" by Kevin Sherwood and Elena Siegman

There are four masks available in Rex Infernus, all the masks can be used within the same game and each mask can be removed and placed on a specific pedestal to easily swap between them all.

Horned Skull Mask (Stone Cold Strongold x Ring of Fire)

  1. Remain inside Her House for at least 9 rounds.
  2. Melee the painting on the right side of the chimney to collect the Horned Skull Mask.
    • Note: Meleeing the painting after 5-6 rounds will reward with 600 Essence or some Ammo while after 7-8 rounds it will be either a free Perk can or a Rare Aether Tool.

The Horned Skull Mask offers a damage and armor boost while standing still, with a circle that will damage any enemy entering it. Note: There is a cooldown of 1 minute between each use. The mask can be placed on the pedestal inside Veytharion Sanctuary.

Cowboy Hat Mask (Spider Immunity)

  1. Ride a Web Mother next to the Pack-a-Punch machine and kill around 50 enemies. Note: Riding the Web Mother is time-limited and may require several tries.
  2. Once enough enemies have been killed, the Web Mother will jump into the Pack-a-Punch dying instantly and dropping the Cowboy Hat Mask.

The Cowboy Hat Mask offers some immunity against Deathspinners and Web Mothers. The mask can be placed on the pedestal inside Nyxara Inner Sanctum.

Astronaut Helmet Mask (Longer-lasting Void Claw)

  1. Collect six parts of Mr Peeks:
    • The head is on a shelf in Her House next to Quick Revive.
    • A part is against a rock pillar just outside of Caltheris' Passage on the right side; requires the Void Claw.
    • A part is located on the other side of the cliff opposite of the Mystery Box location in Ruinas Insula; requires the Void Claw.
    • A part is located floating over an edge below the main ground and a platform in Aranea Insula; requires the Void Claw.
    • A part is located on top of a rock pillar above Juggernog in front of the higher platform in Spira Insula; requires the Void Claw.
    • A part is located outside of Nexus Forge, in-between Veytharion's and Dravakar's temples; requires the Void Claw.
  2. Make sure all the doors around the map are open.
  3. Travel to Caltheris Main Chamber and look for Mr Peeks on the ledge of the platform above the Mystery Box location.
  4. Interact with Mr Peeks to start a Grappling Hook parkour course. Cross every Mr Peeks circle until it reaches the doorway of Nyxara Entrance.
  5. Completing the parkour course in 50 seconds or less will drop the Astronaut Helmet, alongside other loot such as a Raw Aetherium Crystal.
    • Note: Complete the parkour course will reward with loot such as Essence, Salvaga and a Mystery Perk while completing it under a minute will aware a Ray Gun.

The Astronaut Helmet offers an increased 30 seconds of use for the Void Claw and Void Talon. The mask can be placed on the pedestal in Caltheris Main Chamber on the platform above the Mystery Box location.

Spiked Skull Mask (Twins Ammo effect)

  1. Travel to the forest from Nyxara Inner Sanctum.
    • Note: This can be done once per round after the fruit has spawned.
  2. Interact with the music box located below a fallen tree trunk and leave the forest.
  3. Return to the forest and interact with a blue toy box located between two trees.
  4. Three items will appear above the toy box, interacting with an item will trigger a mini-game by the Twins:
    • Teddy Bear: Collect glowing plushies while being pursued by enemies who will move only when you're not looking before the time runs out.
    • Mask: Quickly find the Twins before the time runs out. Note: When the Twins are found, their energy will move towards the direction of their next spot.
    • Rope: Do a skipping rope game, failing 3 times will end the mini-game.
  5. Completing the Mask mini-game will reward with 1000 Essence; completing the Rope mini-game will reward with 600 Essence; and completing the Teddy Bear mini-game will reward with 600 Essence.
    • Note: Completing all the mini-games will reward with possible Aether Tool, Aetherium Crystal, Mystery Perk, and Wonder Weapon.
  6. Return to the forest and complete all three mini-games once more. However, the min-games will be harder to complete this time around
    • Teddy Bear: Zombies are faster with less time to complete.
    • Mask: Being stunned every time the Twins are found.
    • Rope: Longer with more variation, failing 2 times will end the mini-game.
  7. Completing the harder version of all the mini-games will reward with the same kind of loot as the normal version with the Spiked Skull Mask also becoming available.

The Spiked Skull Mask offers a Twins ammo effect for your gun, the Twins will appear on the ground like a lightning strike kill all the zombies nearby. The mask can be placed on the pedestal inside Dravakar Sanctuary, on the left of the Armor wall-buy and doorway.
