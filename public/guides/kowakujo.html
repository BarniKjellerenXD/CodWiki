Kowakujō | Complete Map Breakdown

This guide is broken down into Chapters and Topics, a Chapter defines the content of the Topics to make all of the guides easier to navigate and read.

On PC, there is a list of all the Chapters on the right. Just click on the text and you will be taken there. Alternatively, to find specific information, press CTRL + F and type the information you want to find. On mobile, the list of all the Chapters and Topics is at the top of the page.

On the fractured slopes of a volcanic landscape, a castle harbors a smouldering mystery. Face the Dragon's undead army, investigate the Shogun's fate, and expose the curse that blackened Takeo's soul.

Key Features

This is a walkthrough of the key features of the map, such as Pack-a-Punch, Wonder Weapons, etc.

  1. Build the Maneki-Neko at the bench near Vulture Aid in Workshop.
    • Cat Statue — Kitchens or Gatehouse.
    • Bell — Sinking House in Stables or hanging above the Warden wallbuy.
    • Robot Figurine — Storage/Workshop.
  2. With PhD Flopper equipped, dive to prone below the suspended cage at Tenshu Entrance to drop it.
  3. Pick up the cage and throw it into lava, the cage should melt and a Nekomata will run away.
  4. Get a kill at the entrance of one of the doorways until glowing paw prints spawn on the ground.
    • Entrance of Flower Garden going into Kitchens
    • Entrance of Kitchens going into Central Courtyard
    • Entrance of Stables going into Staging Area
    • Entrance of Training Area going into Stables
  5. The paws will point towards a direction, walk a few steps in that direction get another kill. If done correctly, a new set of paws will appear on the ground.
  6. Repeat the step until you find a triangular shape made of paw prints.
  7. Throw the Maneki-Neko in the middle of the shape. If done correctly, the Nekomata will jump out of the lava. Note: the lava must not be cooled during that step.
  8. An Abomination will spawn and eat the Nekomata. Kill the Abomination to free it.
  9. Wait the next round, and the Nekomata will appear somewhere near the creaky floorboards in either Kitchens or Training Area. The cat will be sleeping and purring.
  10. Sneak on the Nekomata by avoiding the floorboards, which will be highlighted if Death Perception is equipped, and pick it up.
  11. Bring the Nekomata to the World Seed next to the Pack-a-Punch machine. If done correctly, it will jump on the seed.
  12. Wait until the seed traps the Nekomata and pulses red. Each pulsation will push you back and damage you. Keep rushing and meleeing the seed fast enough until the Nekomata is freed.
  13. If done correctly, the Nekomata will jump on the floor and can be picked up as the Nekomancer.

Main Quest

One-screen quick reference. Full details for every step are in the sections below.

① Setup

  1. Build & upgrade the Maneki-Neko, obtain the Nekomancer.
  2. Lantern Step: shoot 11 lanterns (timed!) → spirit corpse in War Room → Ceramic Shards.
  3. Mask Step: Ninja Kite → Fox Mask → Simon Says ×3 → blue mask.

② Evidences — 🎭 Suspect 🤝 Accomplice

  1. Suspect: hide-and-seek mask → 3 items (Pipe, Comb, Case) → Meditation Room shelves.
  2. Accomplice — Merchant: Coin Purse at spawn → apple zombie → kill at merchant window → Abacus.
  3. Gardener: fill Bucket (3 waters!) → water 3 plants → shoot glowing flowers → Shears.
  4. Noble: 3 decoys at windows → reflect grenades → Hat.

③ Evidences — ☠️ Poison

  1. Monkshood: Empty Pouch → Plant + Volcanic Ash (fissure) → pot + tornado → flower.
  2. Plum Pit: punch fruit in Kitchens → jump until gold seed.
  3. Pufferfish: need Pestle → kitchen bowl soul box → ghost → fish.

④ Evidences — 📍 Location 🗡️ Motive

  1. Location (need Saka Cup): 3 soul boxes → War Room, Collapse Study, Tea Garden (chase dog).
  2. Motive: Netsuke of Brothers (Stables statue) + Hanko (first Oni).
  3. Clock Puzzle: storage clock ×4 → assault wave → Japanese numbers → flags into bamboo shoots → final evidence. 🧮 solver

⑤ Key Items & Murder Puzzle

  1. Saka Cup: repair ceramic shards at Tea Garden table.
  2. Pestle: scroll pattern in Storage Rooms → Doctor's Note.
  3. Paintings 1–5: place matching evidence by each painting; use trap ghost (accomplice), Doctor's Note + dial (poison), background match (location).

Witness Testimony & Zodiac Dial are part of the murder puzzle — full details in the Painting steps below.

⑥ Endgame

  1. Purify World Seed: follow light → mini-boss → dragon takes seed. (Perfect run → Katana in PaP room.)
  2. Expel the Onryo: incense bowls → glowing orb → Shogun's Sanctum → World Seed → Onryo fight (shoot its masks to break immunity).
  3. Nyxara: 3 fishes on pole in PaP room → fast travel → volcano boss.

Lantern Step

  1. With the Nekomancer shoot at 11 lanterns located around the map. Note: There is a timer to complete this step. (Easiest to start from Kitchens and do a circle.)
    • Tensu — Near Bath entrance
    • Central Courtyard — Left side of the tree
    • Central Courtyard — Near Kitchen entrance
    • Kitchen — Near stairs
    • Flower Garden — Near Gobblegum machine
    • Gatehouse — Near Speed Cola
    • Outer Ward — Near Mystery Box spawn
    • Staging Area — Near hillside to Central Courtyard
    • Stables — Near Gobblegum machine
    • Training Area — Out of bounds near Melee Macchiato
    • Tea Garden — Near Mystery Box spawn
  2. If done correctly, a spirit corpse with a katana embedded in it will spawn in War Room. Interact with it to trigger a short in-game cinematic.
  3. Pick up the Ceramic Shards from where the spirit corpse was.

Mask Step

  1. Use the Ninja Kite (glider above where you crafted the Maneki-Neko), hold to the right and use the interact button to pick up the Fox Mask hanging from the end of one of the roof edges.
  2. Bring the Fox Mask to the wall with the other masks in Storage Rooms.
  3. Interact with the wall to start a Simon Says-like game. Some of the masks will glow one by one then zombies wearing the same masks will appear. Kill the zombies in the same order as the masks glowed.
  4. Repeat the process 3 times. If done correctly, the Fox Mask will turn blue and interacting with it will trigger an in-game cinematic.

The Fox Mask will move and hide around the map (Hide and Seek Mask Step). Shoot the mask to drop an evidence and pick each one:

  • Takeshi's Pipe: Bottom shelf beside the Workshop stairs.
  • Mitsuhime's Comb: Wunderfizz ceiling.
  • Takeo's Case: Far-left Elemental Pop window.

Place all three items on the shelves in Meditation Room. If done correctly, the painting of a person wearing the fox mask will appear on the other side of the room.

Merchant (Abacus)
  1. Find the Mystery Box location with the gold Japanese plaque at its base. If the Mystery Box isn't there, keep moving it until it spawns (Fire Sale helps).
  2. Place the Maneki-Neko by the side of the Mystery Box near the symbol and use the box (spin for a gun — it will give you the Coin Purse instead).
  3. Pick up the Coin Purse.
  4. Go to Outer Ward (spawn) and place the Coin Purse on the right of Rampage Inducer, then interact with it again to trigger a short in-game cinematic. (Must activate the Coin Purse at spawn before proceeding!)
  5. In Kitchens, punch the apple in the basket so it falls onto the floor. Leave it there.
  6. Within 1–2 rounds a zombie will spawn eating the apple. Lead it to the merchant window in the Staging Area.
  7. Knife the zombie in front of the bloody window with the Nekomancer Wonder Weapon.
  8. The Merchant will appear. Kill him quickly and collect the Abacus evidence + note.
Gardener (Garden Shears)
  1. Collect the Bucket from:
    • Inside a cupboard in Tea Garden
    • Top floor of Onsen Baths
  2. Interact with either pool to fill the Bucket. Note: The Bucket contains 3 parts of water. Running, sprinting, getting hit will remove one part each time.
  3. Water the three sprouted plants that appear on the gravel path in Flower Garden. They only appear after completing the Coin Purse step.
  4. Hold interact on each patch.
  5. A Gardener zombie will spawn. He is immune until he waters all three plants.
  6. Two flowers will begin glowing — shoot the glowing flowers with a charged Nekomancer shot to remove his immunity.
  7. Kill the Gardener and collect the Gardening Shears evidence + note.
Noble (Noble's Hat)
  1. Go to the Training Area. Above the entrance to the Tea Garden there are 3 windows with shutter boards.
  2. Throw 3 decoys at the window — a noble zombie will spawn in the window throwing grenades.
  3. Reflect the grenades back at the noble to kill him. Strategy: hold the grenade to cook it and time your throw to hit him.
  4. Once dead, he will drop the Noble's Hat evidence + note.

Monkshood Flower
  1. Go to the room next to Melee Macchiato with the crafting table and ammo box. Shoot the top section of the broken doorway (facing the large tree) with a charged Nekomancer shot. Collect the Empty Pouch.
  2. Find the Plant beside the broken bridge near the crafting table — look in the cracks on the right-hand side of the rocks.
  3. When a Fissure becomes active on the map, stay near it and use the Empty Pouch to collect Volcanic Ash.
  4. Plant the Plant in the pot near the Exfil entrance in Tenshu Entrance. Pour the Volcanic Ash onto the planted plant.
  5. Punch a cherry blossom tree three times to gain the glowing effect, then return to the pot and spin around in a circle until a small tornado appears.
  6. If done correctly, the Monkshood Flower evidence can be picked up.
Plum Pit
  1. Travel to Kitchens and punch the green fruit in the basket near the fire pit. If done correctly, a plum should drop on the ground.
  2. Jump on the green fruit until a gold seed spawns — collect the Plum Pit evidence.

Horse Statue
  1. Go to the War Room — in the back left of the room by the map, there is a tea bag on paper on the floor.
  2. Interact to start a pulsing yellow light soul box. Kill zombies until complete.
  3. Once done, the Horse Statue evidence will spawn by the table behind you.
Collapse Study
  1. Go to Collapse Study — in the back left by the Wunderfizz machine, interact to spawn a pulsing yellow light soul box.
  2. Kill zombies until the soul box is complete.
  3. Location evidence will spawn.
Tea Garden
  1. Go to the Tea Garden, in the area where you collect the bucket. Interact to spawn a pulsing yellow light soul box.
  2. Kill zombies until the soul box is complete.
  3. A dog may spawn and take the evidence item and run away — chase the dog and kill it to collect the final location evidence item.

Netsuke of Brothers
  1. When the lava blacks out in the Stables, a small statue appears.
  2. Throw a Molotov or explosive at the statue to knock it to the ground.
  3. Collect the Netsuke of Brothers evidence.
Hanko
  1. Kill the first regular Oni that spawns (this is the Hanko Oni — not the Onryo boss from the endgame).
  2. Collect the Shogun's Hanko evidence that drops.
Clock Puzzle (Final Motive Evidence)
  1. Make sure all previous evidence is placed in the evidence room.
  2. Go to the Storage Rooms — left of the entrance by the rubble you will see a clock.
  3. Interact with the clock 4 times to get it to spin, revealing a 4-digit code.
  4. This triggers an assault wave — complete it as normal.
  5. 4 blue numbers will appear around the map in Japanese for the code order:
    • 一 (1) — Stables
    • 二 (2) — Flower Garden
    • 三 (3) — Central Courtyard
    • 四 (4) — Spawn / Outer Ward
  6. Around the map where the bamboo shoots were, flags have spawned with symbols (e.g., 3 diamonds, 4 squares). Pick up the flags to create your number. (Example: if your first number is 6, find 2 flags with 3 symbols each, or a flag with 4 symbols and a flag with 2 symbols.)
  7. There are bamboo shoots in each area with no flag — place your flags in these shoots.
  8. Go back to the clock and interact. If done correctly, it will open to give you the last piece of motive evidence.

🧮 Clock & Flags Solver — enter the 4-digit code

Both key items are picked up inside their evidence steps — jump to the step that needs them:

  • Saka Cup — collect it in the #4 — Location Evidences step (collapsible card at its start).

Two of the paintings have sub-steps — the Witness Testimony (Painting 2) and the Zodiac Dial (Painting 3) — which are covered below.

🧮 Murder Mystery Solver — accomplice, poison & dial

Painting 1 (Leftmost) — Place the Comb in front of the first painting.

Painting 2 (Accomplice) — Whichever ghost you identify through the Witness Testimony (Gardener, Merchant, or Noble) determines which evidence you place. Put that evidence in front of the second painting.

Witness Testimony

This identifies the accomplice for Painting 2. Go to one of the Ghostly Riflemen Traps and activate it. After about 10 to 20 kills, a Ghostly Fireman will appear:

  • Entrance between Outer Ward and Gatehouse; the Ghostly Rifleman will be behind the trap near the house against the wall.
  • Entrance between Flower Garden and Kitchens; the Ghostly Rifleman will be in Kitchens in the upper room near the window.
  • Entrance between Central Courtyard and Tenshu Entrance; the Ghostly Rifleman will be behind the trap near the door.

While the trap is active, approach the Ghostly Fireman so he can talk and reveal the accomplice:

  • "That merchant from the market was hanging around..."

However, if the Ghostly Fireman doesn't mention anyone, that means another trap needs to be activated. The ghost you see confirms which accomplice evidence to use.

Painting 3 (Poison) — Using the Doctor's Note (from the Pestle step), it will tell you how he died and by what poison. Match the correct poison to the note and place it by the third painting. The note also gives the time of death, which you set on the Zodiac Dial:

Zodiac Dial

  1. Check the Death Records and note the "Time of Death" which will display the name of a Zodiac animal as "Hour of the [Animal]".
  2. Note: All possible animals are Dog, Boar, Rat, Ox, Tiger and Hare.
  3. Check the note with the poison list and note the time it takes for the corresponding item on the cloth to kill someone.
  4. Place the dial hand on the animal from the Death Records, then, like a normal clock, remove the time from poison list.
  5. Example: If the Time of Death is "Hour of the Hare" and the time from the poison is "3 hours", place the hand on the hare then move it by 3 cases anti-clockwise (from right to left) which should correspond to the rat.

Painting 4 (Location) — There is a painting hanging nearby (fish, mountain, etc.) which will match with the background of the 4th painting. Whatever evidence you found in that area is the one you place.

Painting 5 (Rightmost) — Place the Clock Seal in front of the fifth painting.

If done correctly a glowing light will spawn that you have to follow.

  2. Interact with it to purify it — this takes you to a mini-boss fight arena.
  3. Defeat the mini-boss and you will be taken back to the map.
  4. The dragon will spawn in and take the World Seed away.
  5. Note: If you complete the evidence steps correctly on the first attempt, a Katana will spawn in the Pack-a-Punch room. If you fail and have to redo, it will not appear.

  1. Interact with the incense bowls on either side of the paintings. If done correctly, a glowing orb will appear.
  2. Kill zombies near the orb until it moves to Shogun's Sanctum.
  3. Interact with the World Seed to start the fight against the Onryo.
  4. After being teleported to the Central Courtyard a special Oni will appear as the Onryo. Periodically, the Onryo will become immune and shooting its masks will remove that immunity.
  5. Once the Onryo is killed, a fast travel will appear in Shogun's Sanctum to travel to the Volcano and fight Nyxara.
  6. Additionally, if the mystery was solved without any mistake, an Ultra-rarity Path of Sorrows will drop in the room and can be picked up for the Melee slot.

Relics

There are 6 Relics that can be unlocked from playing Kowakujō, those Relics are then used as gameplay modifiers for Cursed mode.

The Relics are divided into three groups: Grim, Sinister, and Wicked. Each group fill the Cursed Tier bar differently with the Grim Relics filling up a third of the bar, the Sinister Relics filling up two thirds, and the Wicked Relics filling up the entire bar. As such you will need to apply different Relics to reach the desired Tier level.

  • Tier I: Any 3 Grim — Unlocks Golden Armor (45,000 Essence)
  • Tier II: 2 Sinister + 2 Grim (example loadout) — Unlocks Ultra Rarity (8,000 Salvage or 17,500 Salvage from default rarity)
  • Tier III: 3 Grim + 2 Sinister + 1 Wicked (example) — Unlocks Pack-a-Punch IV (100,000 Essence)

Make sure to scroll sideways to see the steps. Higher tiers just need the total difficulty value to match the target fill; swap relics as you discover them.

Grim Relics

GRAMOPHONE Bullets deal increased damage but each shot consumes 2 bullets

  2. Go to the drums in the stairs from Flower Garden inside the Kitchen — melee both drums, then press interact on them to start Simon Says.
  3. Play Simon Says: shoot or melee the drums in the order shown to you. You must do this 3 times before the portal opens.
Portal: Kitchens
Trial: Only sniper damage counts to kill enemies. The only sniper wallbuy is the XR3 Ion across the room from Pack-a-Punch (a shotgun attachment on the Shadow SK also works).
Tips: Doable with the XR3 Ion — use Free Fire + Insta-Kill for the last 2 HVTs; it's relatively easy.
DRUID STONE No bleed out bar self-revives instantly revives you
✅ Any Cursed tier
  1. Survive until round 20 without taking damage from zombies.
  2. Walking in lava is fine — you just cannot get hit by zombies (similar to the Social Distancing Dark Ops, but slightly different).
  3. Cheese (until patched): play a Squad No-Fill game and invite a player on round 19, then reach round 20. Solo: beat the boss, save & quit as soon as you return from the boss fight, then reload the save.
Portal: Flower Garden
Trial: Zombies can only take damage when you are overhealed.
Tips: Light Mend on your weapon, Cherry Blossoms (pink trees — melee 3× for overcharged health), or Stim Shots.

  1. Complete the Path of Torment part of the main EE to get Takeo's Sword — do the main EE up until the boss fight.
