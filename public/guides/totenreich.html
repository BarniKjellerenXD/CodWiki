Totenreich | Complete Map Breakdown

This guide is broken down into Chapters and Topics, a Chapter defines the content of the Topics to make all of the guides easier to navigate and read.

On PC, there is a list of all the Chapters on the right. Just click on the text and you will be taken there. Alternatively, to find specific information, press CTRL + F and type the information you want to find. On mobile, the list of all the Chapters and Topics is at the top of the page.

In the frozen birthplace of nuclear fire, a Group 935 research base hides sinister secrets. Awaken the steel colossus, ignite the flame of vengeance, and free the giant Shadowsmith from the Warden's icy grip!

Key Features

This is a walkthrough of the key features of the map, such as Pack-a-Punch, Wonder Weapons, etc.

  1. Get 3 items:
    1. Chilly Chunks - Back of a truck next to Deadshot Daiquiri in Storm Bridge.
    2. Lantern - Place the Chilly Chunks on the table in the center of Skallen Market. Starting with the 2nd Special Round, a named Zursa will spawn and will drop the Lantern after being killed.
    3. Chain Links - Interact with the button in Titan Crane to lower a wooden slab in front of the ship. Wall jump towards the bow of the ship and interact to get the chains.
      • Note: the slab will only stay down for 30 seconds, but the button can be interacted as many times as needed.
  2. Reach the Burial Grounds and interact with the door to put the chain links in, then hold interact to repair the door.
  3. Place the Lantern on the shrine in Burial Grounds. The lantern will emit lights on the wall, revealing constellations that rotate.
  4. Interact with the constellation symbols on the wall, based on the ones on the shrine at Burial Grounds
    • The order is Left, Right, Back, Front when looking at the shrine from the stairs.
  5. If done correctly, the ghost of Astrid will appear above the shrine.
  6. Follow Astrid. She will move toward the market, then stop. Kill Frost zombies that spawn to charge her energy bar until it is filled.
  7. Once Astrid is near the lighthouse, repeat the energy charging process once more. Once done, floating platforms will appear. Climb the platforms and reach the lighthouse.
  8. Continue to climb the lighthouse with the stairs and the outside platforms.
  9. Interact with the ladder on top of the lighthouse to enter the top chamber, where the Jotunn Star will be available for pickup.

Main Quest

One-screen quick reference. Full details for every step are in the sections below.

① Setup

  1. Obtain the Jotunn Star (wonder weapon).

② Wundersignal

  1. Crowbar in Lighthouse; find the note (Dry Dock / Tyr's Foot) → read the crate code: III-6, V-7, X-9, or IV-3.
  2. Open the matching crate → Flak Gun Round. (Wrong crate = 30s penalty.)
  3. Load the flak gun near the Lighthouse, shoot the Riese head in spawn.
  4. Take Transmitter from Riese head → place in Tyr's head (2nd floor).
  5. Count the 2 sets of blinking lights → 2×2-digit numbers.
  6. Core Foundry Ascender (Molotov) → console: calibrate Amplitude + Frequency → fire both sets → Wunderbarrage Controller.

③ Atomkraft Core — 3 Uraniums

  1. U-1: Fishing Rod → glowing green fish → Irradiated Ravager next round → kill for Uranium.
  2. U-2: ARC-XD melee vent → secret room → head jars A–E + radioactive symbols → Necrospike lockpick → Uranium.
  3. U-3: Glocke Drop → shoot floating zombies, light 3 green lights → Uranium.
  4. Clear debris (Wunderbarrage) → Machine Workshop → claw machine: 6&3 / 7&2 / 7&1&1 → Atomkraft Core.
  5. Defend generator (Quick Revive pallets) → Charged Core to Storm Bridge barrel → Dravakar Shard.

④ Vegvisir

  1. Place shard on Blodheim Hall bonfire → light with Jotunn Star ranged attack.
  2. Disciple Injection → throw zombies into fire → lockdown → kill Necropincer HVT → Sunstone.
  3. Sunstone in Eidskallen Stave Church center → giant arrow circle → shoot rune wood piles in order (arrow count = order).
  4. Return to Tyr's Head console → boss fight.

⑤ Dravakar Boss

  1. Shoot red weak spots while Tyr wrestles; ice attacks freeze areas → use 3 Flammenfalle traps.
  2. Tyr disabled → Gjallarfrost heads: grenade in mouth or Jotunn Star ranged to stun → destroy; dodge ice shard runes, shoot random weak spots.
  3. More Gjallarfrost heads; middle area locked, no cover — keep DPS on weak spots until health bar empties.

Use the Ascender and enter the console to calibrate the Amplitude (right) and Frequency (left) using the numbers from the blinking lights. Enter the first set of numbers which will launch a barrage strike somewhere on the map, then enter the second set of numbers.
  8. If done correctly, a free Wunderbarrage Controller will spawn in the room next to the radio tower. The controller will also become available at crafting tables.

  1. Obtain three Uraniums, which can be done in any order:
    • Uranium 1
      1. Find a Fishing Rod (Dry Dock, Storm Bridge, Fishery Island, Beacon Island)
      2. Find a glowing green fish jumping in the water at one of the fishing location and use the Fishing Rod. Catching the fish will spawn an Irradiated Ravager.
      3. Wait for the next round, and it will appear somewhere on the map, alongside a horde of regular Ravagers. Killing the Irradiated Ravager will drop the Uranium.
    • Uranium 2
      1. Get an ARC-XD and melee the vent near stairs and Zipline in Core Foundry. With the ARC-XD jump on the boat on the other side and enter the secret room to destroy the barrels and open the Group 935 Genetic Lab.
        • Note: a free ARC-XD can be picked up in the Eidskallen Square, and it can also be acquired randomly from fishing.
      2. There will be 5 head jars marked A to E around the room. Look at the corridor next to the locked cell door and note the radioactive symbols beneath the numbers (1 to 5). The numbers match the letter on the jars (A=1 ... E=5) and the radioactive symbols show which jar is needed. Pick up the corresponding jars and place them in the machine next to the cell to get the glowing purple jar.
      3. Interact with the crab arm on the table to pick up the Necrospike. Use the Necrospike on the cell door to trigger a lockpicking mini-game.
      4. If done correctly, the cell door will open and the Uranium can be picked up. Note: a radio can also be picked up on the ground beneath the bench for a Relic.
    • Uranium 3 - Note: This Uranium can be acquired without the need to build the Jotunn Star or start the main quest.
      1. Obtain the Glocke Drop and activate it.
        • Note: a free drop is available as part of the beginning steps of the main quest, and it can also be acquired randomly from fishing.
      2. While active, shoot all the floating zombies to turn on the green light. Keep doing it until all 3 green lights are lit. If done correctly, the Glocke will drop the Uranium.
  2. Use the Wunderbarrage Controller to clear the debris on the stairs outside of 02 Building in Dry Dock. Climb the stairs and enter the Machine Workshop. The door to the other side will then automatically open.
  3. Place all 3 Uraniums inside the claw machine and interact with it.
  4. Complete the mini-game by placing the cylinders in slots in a way that the cursor does not go over the green section on screen.The goal is to create several groups by placing the three Uranium tubes in specific places. The configurations can either be: 6 & 3; 7 & 2; or 7 & 1 & 1. 🧮 Uranium Pincers solver.
  5. If done correctly, the Atomkraft Core will drop.
    • Note that you cannot sprint while holding the Atomkraft Core and using a zipline or entering Tyr will drop it on the ground.
  6. Place the Atomkraft Core on pallets next to Quick Revive then turn on the generator in the shed behind the perk. You will need to defend the Atomkraft Core. During the defense, the generator will occasionally require rebooting to progress.
  7. Once done, bring the Charged Atomkraft Core to Storm Bridge and place it on a barrel. If done correctly, a cutscene will play and the Dravakar Shard will drop next to Tyr's Foot.

  1. Bring the shard inside the Blodheim Hall and place it on the bonfire. Lit the bonfire with the ranged attack of the Jotunn Star.
  2. Get a Disciple Injection and throw zombies inside the bonfire. Note: a free Disciple Injection can be found in the Eidskallen Stave Church.
  3. If done correctly, this should trigger a lockdown and spawn an Necropincer HVT.
  4. After killing the HVT, the Sunstone can be picked up.
  5. Place the Sunstone in the center of Eidskallen Stave Church and use the Jotunn Star's range attack on it. A giant circle will spawn above the church with arrows pointing at different runes.
  6. Runes will spawn around the map, shoot the wooden pile below each rune with the range attack of the Jotunn Star in the order shown in the giant circle, based on the number of arrow points per rune.
  7. Once done, return to Tyr's Head and interact with the console to start the boss fight

Note: Throughout the duration of this fight, if players have completed the Astra Malorum main quest, a power-up called Caltheris's Blessing will drop when all players go down and have no revives left. It will revive the downed player who picks it up.

  1. At the start of the fight, Dravakar will wrestle with Tyr in the background. At certain points, red weak spots will glow on Dravakar; deal damage to these weak spots until they break, which will allow Tyr to gain the upper hand. Otherwise, Dravakar will focus on the players and release an ice attack to one of the three areas in the arena, causing it to freeze temporarily. Three Flammenfalle traps are placed around the arena, which can be used to counter the effects of the ice attacks.
  2. After dealing enough damage to Dravakar, he will disable Tyr for the rest of the fight. A Gjallarfrost head will spawn in the middle area, which will attack players when spotted. Stun the Gjallarfrost by throwing a grenade in its mouth, or using the ranged attack of the Jotunn Star, then deal damage with any weapon until it is destroyed. Dravakar will then appear and summon ice shards in rune patterns and shoot them at the players; hide behind covers to avoid the shards, or study the rune patterns to dodge them. During this phase, Dravakar's body will have random red glowing weak spots that can be shot at.
  3. In later phases, Dravakar will summon additional Gjallarfrost heads on other sides of the arena. Eventually he will also block off access to the other areas, forcing the player to stay in the middle area with no cover from the ice shard attacks. Continue dealing damage to Dravakar's weak spots until his health bar is depleted.

Relics

There are 6 Relics that can be unlocked from playing Totenreich, those Relics are then used as gameplay modifiers for Cursed mode.

The Relics are divided into three groups: Grim, Sinister, and Wicked. Each group fill the Cursed Tier bar differently with the Grim Relics filling up a third of the bar, the Sinister Relics filling up two thirds, and the Wicked Relics filling up the entire bar. As such you will need to apply different Relics to reach the desired Tier level.

  • Tier I: Any 3 Grim — Unlocks Golden Armor (45,000 Essence)
  • Tier II: 2 Sinister + 2 Grim (example loadout) — Unlocks Ultra Rarity (8,000 Salvage or 17,500 Salvage from default rarity)
  • Tier III: 3 Grim + 2 Sinister + 1 Wicked (example) — Unlocks Pack-a-Punch IV (100,000 Essence)

Make sure to scroll sideways to see the steps. Higher tiers just need the total difficulty value to match the target fill; swap relics as you discover them.
