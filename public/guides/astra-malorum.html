LGM-1

  1. Collect several items:
    1. Destroy one of O.S.C.A.R. flying drones to obtain the Damaged Drone.
    2. Find a blinking lamp post around the map and shoot it to drop the Aberrant Wiring.
    3. Equipped with a Pack-a-Punched gun, shoot the gearbox of Ol' Tessie in Crash Site to collect the Car Battery.
    4. With Cryofreeze equipped, find and shoot an Aether Crystal around the map to drop an Absolute Zero Shard. You need to collect a total of 3 shards.
  2. Kill O.S.C.A.R. using three different traps. This can be done in any order, but only one can be done per round.
    1. Place the Absolute Zero Shard inside the rocket thrusters in Museum Infinitum. If done correctly, a countdown will be heard. Activate the trap and bring O.S.C.A.R. to the rocket thrusters to kill it.
    2. Place the Damaged Drone inside the Electro-Volt Projector at the Luminarium. Bring O.S.C.A.R. inside the room and interact with the trap to kill it.
    3. Inside the Observatory Dome, find and shoot at 9 fuming valves around the room. If done correctly, the telescope will start moving.
      • Two are located above the telescope.
      • Two are located under the telescope.
      • Three are located on the walls inside the room.
      • One is located on a pillar behind a sparking box.
      • One is located in the small room when coming from Machina Astralis
    4. Bring O.S.C.A.R. beneath the telescope and interact with the organ in front of the Pack-a-Punch machine. A screen will appear and you'll need to place the cursor on the Sun to activate the trap. Make sure O.S.C.A.R. stays within the laser beam to kill it.
  1. Once all three traps are done, the final O.S.C.A.R. killed will drop the LGM-1.

Main Quest

  1. Acquire the LGM-1.
  2. Starting at Round 10. Stay behind O.S.C.A.R. and follow it until it gives the name of three planets. Turn the planets into numbers based on their distance from the Sun. Mercury = 1, Neptune = 8
  3. Travel to the Observatory Dome and enter the 3-digit code into the terminal. If done correctly, a lift will drop down.
  4. Retrieve the key from the corpse on the lift then travel to Machina Astralis and interact with the cryopod. The pod will open revealing a corpse.
  5. Travel to Museum Infinitum and find the Saw located behind a glass display, shoot the glass to pick it up. Return to the pod and interact with the corpse head to collect the brain.
  6. Travel to the Luminarium and interact with the brain on the table to replace it. This will trigger a short lockdown where you need to defend the brain.
  7. Pick the brain up again and bring it to the machine near the teleporter in Machina Astralis. If done correctly, the machine will power up and will display a list of book titles.
  8. Travel to the Observatory Dome and interact with the telescope, this requires to activate the trap or obtain the LGM-1 first. Look for Mars and find its coordinates, this will be a 4-digit code.
  9. Return to the machine and enter the code. If done correctly, the machine will say that the coordinates are for Mars.
  10. Travel to the Archive of Orbis. Inside the room, there will be three busts of woman. There are a set of 3 bookshelves next to each bust. Each bookshelf contains a book. Find the book titles displayed by the machine. You will need to interact with each bust as many times as the number of corresponding book titles found in their set of bookshelves. You will have a very short time to interact with all 3 busts at the start of a round, it is recommended to note all the book titles first, then pass the round and interact with the busts.
  1. If done correctly, a panel will open above the ladder in the room. Climb it to retrieve the Neptune model and place it in the display at the top of the Machina Astralis.
  2. Find three notes around the map that display a direction (eg. NE) and planet (Mars)
    • One note can be found on a desk in the Luminarium
    • One note can be found on a table in Machina Astralis
    • One note can be found on a table in Archive of Orbis
  3. Shoot the corresponding planets on the model display to align each of them in the direction mentioned in the notes. The compass showing the directions is located around the Sun model.
  4. Return to Machina Astralis and interact with the brain to reboot the portal. This will spawn O.S.C.A.R. and you'll have to defend the portal until it has rebooted. If done correctly, you will be teleported on Mars.
  5. While on Mars, pick up the brain located in the center of the arena and place it in the machine at the top of the stairs. Interact with it to create a portal. Step 6 can be done as you create the portal.
  6. Interact with the machine to play a sound. This can be done once per round. When the sound is played, the ascendant eye will be seen flying near the giant head outside the map.
  7. While the ascendant eye is flying, shoot at the four pylons outside of the map in this order: Back Right, Back Left, Front Right, Front Left, then shoot at the pylon located above the machine, within the temple. If done correctly, the ascendant will fly to the center of the arena then fly randomly to a lower part of the arena. When it's close, jump and grab it.
  8. Place the ascendant eye in the device on the right side of the arena, backtowards where you shot the pylons.
  9. Using the LGM-1, shoot at the antennas of the five pillars found around the arena, if done correctly, a song will be played and a symbole will be displayed on the pillar.
  10. Travel back to the Observatory Dome and interact with the organ. The little screen on the right side will display the corresponding five symbols in a specific order. However, one of the symbols will be missing take note of the missing symbol it's place in the order is signified by the wavy lines.
  11. Return to Mars and interact with the corresponding pillars in the order shown on the organ's screen. If done correctly, the screen will bright up and a quote from Thurston will play.
  12. Interact with the machine at the top of the stairs to start the fight against Caltheris.

Caltheris Boss Fight

  1. Kill zombies while dodging the boss's lasers to charge the Tesla coil on the right side of the arena; once it's fully charged, interact with the brain to fire a beam and damage the boss. Repeat the process on the left side, during which the boss begins lobbing lava orbs that leave burning pools on the ground. After both sides activate at once, the boss gains an attack that floods the arena with poison gas while spawning poison zombies when the poison spreads across the floor, climb the stairs to avoid taking damage.
  2. The boss reforms into a smaller rock golem for phase two and will begin chasing you around the arena, slapping you into the air and throwing rocks. Damage the boss until a glowing blue weak point appears, then DPS on the weak point while avoiding zombies and the boss's attacks.
  3. The boss reforms into a larger rock golem for phase three, and after a brief immunity period it begins attacking. The fight plays out similarly to phase two but with an additional slam attack that you need to jump into the air to avoid taking damage.
  4. The boss once again reforms into an even larger rock golem for phase four, and the fight continues similarly to phases two and three, but all of its attacks are now empowered. Its rock throw creates purple pools of electricity that deal heavy damage very quickly, and its slam attack hits harder, stuns for longer, and covers a much wider area.

Side Quests and Easter Eggs

Secret song

Find three Mr Peeks headphones around the map:

  • At the bottom shelf, left of the exit door in Luminarium 
  • On a display case in Machina Astralis
  • On the ground next to a wall in Mars

By Avenged Sevenfold

Alpha Omega Game Over Song

Find three records:

  • Inside a wooden box in Machina Astralis in a box on the middle floor
  • At the top of a bookshelf in the Luminarium
  • On a wooden sil above a zombie spawner in the Museum

Bring the records to gramophone in the Luminarium and it will play the Game Over song from Alpha Omega.

 Friendly Ravager

Look for glowing eyes in zombie spawners around the map and throw a shiny trinket at them; the Ravager will eat the trinket and later reappear in another zombie spawn. Repeat this process twice more, and on the third time he will jump out, clap his cymbals together, and become a temporary friendly Ravager.

Relics

There are several Relics that can be unlocked from playing Astra Malorum, those Relics are then used as gameplay modifiers for Cursed mode.

The Relics are divided into three groups: Grim, Sinister, and Wicked. Each group fill the Cursed Tier bar differently with the Grim Relics filling up a third of the bar, the Sinister Relics filling up two thirds, and the Wicked Relics filling up the entire bar. As such you will need to apply different Relics to reach the desired Tier level.

  • Tier I - Golden Armor available (45,000 Essence)
  • Tier II - Ultra Rarity available (8000 Salvage or 17500 Salvage from default rarity)
  • Tier III - Pack-a-Punch Level IV available (100,000 Essence)
