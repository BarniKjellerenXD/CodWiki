In the cold depths of space, a century-old observatory guards a gateway to a dead world. Awaken the astronomer, defeat the mechanical guard, and liberate the celestial Shadowsmith!

Key Features

This is a walkthrough of the key features of the map, such as Pack-a-Punch, Wonder Weapons, etc.

    1. Destroy one of O.S.C.A.R. flying drones to obtain the Damaged Drone.
    2. Find a blinking lamp post around the map and shoot it to drop the Aberrant Wiring.
    3. Equipped with a Pack-a-Punched gun, shoot the gearbox of Ol' Tessie in Crash Site to collect the Car Battery.
    4. With Cryofreeze equipped, find and shoot an Aether Crystal around the map to drop an Absolute Zero Shard. You need to collect a total of 3 shards.

    2. Place the Damaged Drone, Car Battery and Aberrant Wiring inside the trap at the Luminarium to enable the Electro-Volt Projector. Bring O.S.C.A.R. inside the room and interact with the trap to kill it. Players have to hold O.S.C.A.R. within the room until it overheats and explodes. Purple-eyed zombies will also spawn during this step.
    3. Inside the Observatory Dome, find and shoot at 5 fuming valves around the room within a limited time. If done correctly, the telescope will start moving.
      • Two are located above the telescope.
      • Two are located under the telescope.
      • Three are located on the walls inside the room.
      • One is located on a pillar behind a sparking box.
      • One is located in the small room when coming from Machina Astralis
    4. Bring O.S.C.A.R. beneath the telescope and interact with the organ in front of the Pack-a-Punch machine. A screen will appear and you'll need to place the cursor on the Sun to activate the trap. Make sure O.S.C.A.R. stays within the laser beam to kill it.
  1. Once all three traps are done, the final O.S.C.A.R. killed will drop the LGM-1.

Main Quest

Full walkthrough of the Astra Malorum main quest. For a condensed step-by-step version, see the Main Quest Cheat Sheet below.

  1. Acquire the LGM-1 (see Key Features for the full trap sequence).
  2. Starting at Round 10, stay behind O.S.C.A.R. and follow it until it plays a recording, during which Dr. Thurston will recite the name of three planets. Turn the planets into numbers based on their distance from the Sun (Mercury = 1, Venus = 2, ... Neptune = 8). 🧮 code solver
  3. Travel to the Observatory Dome and enter the 3-digit code into the terminal. If done correctly, a lift will drop down next to the Pack-a-Punch.
  4. Retrieve the key from the corpse on the lift, then travel to Machina Astralis and interact with the cryopod located in the adjacent room leading to Scholar’s Way. The pod will open, revealing Dr. Thurston’s corpse. Note that this will summon O.S.C.A.R. and spawn several zombies to your location.
  5. Travel to Museum Infinitum and find the Rock Saw located behind a glass display; shoot the glass to pick it up. Return to the cryopod and interact with the corpse to collect Thurston’s brain. You can pick up the Rock Saw before Step 4. Once the cryopod is opened, hold interact on Thurston’s head to collect his brain — you will be immune to damage during the process.
  6. Travel to The Luminarium and interact with the brain jar on the table to replace the monkey brain with Thurston’s brain. Interact once more, and this will trigger a short lockdown where you need to defend the brain.
  7. Pick the brain jar up and place it in the machine near the portal in Machina Astralis. Holding the jar will slow you down. Hold interact on the machine to connect the jar to it. If done correctly, the machine will power up and display a list of book titles on the left screen (9 possible titles).
  8. Go to the Archive of Orbis. Inside the room there are three busts, each with a set of 3 bookshelves beside it. Interacting with each bookshelf displays one of the book titles. Find the book titles shown by the machine, then as fast as possible, interact with each bust as many times as the number of matching titles found in its set of shelves. Example: if the machine shows The Black Veil, Echoes of Andromeda, Witchlight Codex, Silence at Singularity, interact with bust 1 once, bust 2 once, bust 3 twice.
  9. If done correctly, a panel will open above the ladder in the room. Climb it to retrieve the Neptune model and place it in the display at the top of Machina Astralis.
  10. Find three pages around the map; each page shows a random planet (e.g. Mars) and a random direction (e.g. NE): one on a table in The Luminarium (same room as the Stellar Dissector trap), one on a desk in Machina Astralis, one on the large table in Archive of Orbis.
  11. Shoot the corresponding planets (Mars, Saturn, Neptune) on the model display to align each in the direction from the notes. The compass showing the directions is below the Sun model. 🧮 compass solver
  12. Travel to the Observatory Dome and interact with the telescope. Look for Mars and note its declination (DEC) — this corresponds to a 4-digit code.
  13. On the second floor of Machina Astralis, enter the code into the machine. If done correctly, Thurston will acknowledge the successful code input.
  14. Interact with the machine to reboot the portal. This spawns O.S.C.A.R., who constantly launches flying saucers at the portal to damage it. Destroy the saucers and defend the portal until it finishes rebooting — if it takes too much damage before the timer runs out, the step fails. If done correctly, you will be teleported to Mars automatically.
  15. While on Mars, pick up Thurston’s brain jar in the center of the arena and place it in the machine at the top of the stairs. Interact with it to create a portal. (Step 6 of the cheat sheet can be done as you create the portal.)
  16. Interact with the machine to play music (once per round). When the music plays, the Ascendant Eye will be seen flying near the giant head outside the map.
  17. Shoot the four pylons outside the map in order: Back Right, Back Left, Front Right, Front Left, then the pylon above the machine on top of the temple to bring the Ascendant Eye into the arena. Shooting the temple pylon repeatedly keeps the music playing, forcing the Ascendant Eye to stay.
  18. Wait for the Ascendant Eye to fly low in the arena, then jump and interact to grab it. Easier with low gravity enabled.
  19. Place the Ascendant Eye in the device on the right side of the arena. With the LGM-1, shoot the antennas on the five pillars around the arena — each plays a note and displays a symbol on its pillar.
  20. Back in the Observatory Dome, interact with the organ. The screen on the right displays the five symbols in order, with one hidden behind distorted wave lines. This step can be done immediately after unlocking Pack-a-Punch.
  21. Return to Mars and interact with the pillars in the order shown on the organ’s screen, inserting the missing symbol where indicated. If done correctly, the screen brightens and a quote from Thurston plays.
  22. Interact with the machine at the top of the stairs to start the fight against Caltheris.

  1. Phase 1 — Kill zombies while dodging Caltheris’s lasers to charge the Tesla coil on the right side of the arena. Once fully charged, interact with the machine at the top of the stairs to fire a beam, instantly draining 1/3 of her health bar. Repeat two more times. After each successful attack, Caltheris gains a new attack: meteors covering an area in flames, or toxic gas on the lower area (draining health to exactly 175 HP) while summoning toxic zombies.
  2. Phase 2 — Caltheris reforms into a smaller rock golem and chases you around the arena, slapping you into the air and throwing rocks. Damage her until a glowing blue weak point appears, then hit it for critical damage.
  3. Phase 3 — Caltheris Ascendant. She has an immunity period during which armored zombies spawn (restock on armor plates). Plays like phase 2 but adds a slam attack — jump into the air to avoid damage.
  4. Phase 4 — Caltheris the Needle. All attacks are empowered: rock throws create purple pools of electricity that deal heavy damage, movement speed increases exponentially, and the slam hits harder, stuns longer, and covers a wider area. Deplete her health bar to win.
    • If you have completed the Ashes of the Damned main quest, Veytharion’s Blessing spawns at the top of the arena during this phase — it refills ammo/armor and grants infinite ammo and equipment for a limited duration.

Main Quest Cheat Sheet

Quick reference for players who already know the quest. Full details are in the Main Quest walkthrough above.

Awaken the astronomer

  1. Acquire the LGM-1 (see Key Features).
  2. Starting at Round 10, follow O.S.C.A.R. until it calls out three planets. Convert planets to numbers by their distance from the Sun (Mercury = 1, Neptune = 8).
  3. Enter the 3-digit code at the Observatory Dome terminal; a lift drops with a corpse and a key.
  4. Go to Machina Astralis, open the cryopod with the key to reveal a corpse.
  5. Shoot the glass at Museum Infinitum to grab the Saw, return to the pod, and take the brain.
  6. Place the brain on the table in the Luminarium and defend during the short lockdown.
  7. Bring the brain to the machine near the teleporter in Machina Astralis to power it; it will list book titles.

Power and align the observatory

  1. Use the Observatory Dome telescope (requires trap activation or LGM-1) to find Mars coordinates (4-digit code).
  2. Enter the code at the Machina Astralis machine; it confirms Mars.
  3. In Archive of Orbis, match the three busts to the counted book titles from their shelves (do this at round start for time).
  4. When correct, climb the opened panel to grab the Neptune model and place it atop Machina Astralis.
  5. Collect three planet notes (directions + planet): Luminarium desk, Machina Astralis table, Archive of Orbis table.
  6. Align the planets on the model display to the noted directions (compass around the Sun model).
  7. Return to Machina Astralis, interact with the brain to reboot the portal, defend against O.S.C.A.R.; you will be sent to Mars.

Mars sequence

  1. Pick up the brain at the arena center, place it in the machine up the stairs, and create the portal.
  2. Each round, play the machine sound to spot the ascendant eye; while it flies, shoot pylons: Back Right > Back Left > Front Right > Front Left > temple pylon above the machine. Grab the eye when it dives.
  3. Place the ascendant eye in the device on the right side of the arena (facing where you shot pylons).
  4. With the LGM-1, shoot the antennas of the five arena pillars until each shows a symbol and a song plays.
  5. Back in Observatory Dome, read the organ screen: it shows the five symbols in order with one missing (denoted by wavy lines). Note the missing symbol and its position.
  6. Return to Mars and interact with the pillars in that exact order, inserting the missing symbol where indicated.
  7. Interact with the machine atop the stairs to start the fight against Caltheris.

Caltheris Boss Fight — Phase Cards

Phase 1 - Laser and lava orbs

Kill zombies while dodging lasers to charge the right Tesla coil; fire the brain beam. Repeat on the left; lava orbs leave burning pools.

  • Gas flood + poison zombies: climb the stairs when the floor fills.
Phase 2 - Small golem

Chases, slaps, throws rocks. Damage until a blue weak point appears, then focus fire while kiting zombies.

Phase 3 - Larger golem

Same kit plus slam shockwave. Jump as the slam lands to avoid damage.

Phase 4 - Empowered golem

All attacks hit harder. Rock throw leaves purple electric pools; slam has bigger radius, longer stun—keep moving and jump slams.

Six Relics modify Cursed Mode in Astra Malorum (two per tier). Effects and unlock steps are still undiscovered; use the placeholders below until they are found.

The Relics are divided into three groups: Grim, Sinister, and Wicked. Each fills the Cursed Tier bar differently (Grim ≈ 1, Sinister ≈ 2, Wicked ≈ 3). Mix combinations to reach the tier you want:

  • Tier I: Any 3 Grim — Unlocks Golden Armor (45,000 Essence)
  • Tier II: 2 Sinister + 2 Grim (example) — Unlocks Ultra Rarity
  • Tier III: 3 Grim + 2 Sinister + 1 Wicked (example) — Unlocks Pack-a-Punch IV (100,000 Essence)

Higher tiers just need the total difficulty value to match the target fill; swap relics as you discover them.

Grim Relics

GONG Field Upgrade starts charged, but can only be charged by full power.
  1. Have Tesla Storm as your Field Upgrade, and at least one weapon with Dead Wire.
  2. Starting at Round 20, and every round afterwards, a zombie will spawn with a lightning rod embedded into its body.
  3. Around the map, there will be three doorways with a light bulb above them: The Luminarium (near the brain jar); Observatory Dome (near the room with the portal); and Archive of Orbis (next to the portal room).
  4. Bring the zombie with the lightning rod below the bulb and activate Tesla Storm. Then shoot the zombie to electrocute it with Dead Wire. If done correctly, the electricity will travel to the light bulb.
  5. Once all three are done, look for the green portal outside between Observatory Dome and Stargazer's Courtyard.
  6. Survive 4 waves where zombies can only be damaged with electric damage. Klaus Cores will also spawn there.
SEED Mystery Box is disabled.
  1. Reach Round 20.
  2. A pistol will spawn on the ground somewhere on the map.
  3. Pick the pistol and kill the exact number of enemies as the round you are in (Round 21 = 21 kills, etc). Once it is done, pass the round using any other weapon. Otherwise, start again the next round. The pistol can be Pack-a-Punched and its rarity can be upgraded.
  4. Travel to Observatory Dome and interact with the green portal to start the Relic Trial.
  5. Survive 4 waves with only a Pack-a-Punched pistol.

Sinister Relics

SPIDER FANG Perks costs at machines never decrease.
  1. Activate Cursed Mode Tier I.
  2. Reach Round 40 and defeat O.S.C.A.R. with a melee weapon while Wisp Tea spirit is attacking it. O.S.C.A.R. can be damaged with other way but the killing blow needs to be done with a melee.
  3. Travel to Archive of Orbis and interact with the yellow portal to start the Relic Trial.
  4. Survive 5 waves while all perks are disabled.
MATRYOSHKA DOLL Salvage drop rate halved.
  1. Activate Cursed Mode Tier I.
  2. Open the portal to Mars and reach Round 40.
  3. Place a C4 on each of the three gut piles found on the central platform on Mars.
  4. Gather a group of zombies in the middle of the platform and detonate the C4. If done correctly, Mr Peeks will be heard laughing.
  5. Travel to the side room of Machina Astralis and interact with the yellow portal to start the Relic Trial.
  6. Survive 5 waves while zombies and HVTs can only be killed with Field Upgrades.

Wicked Relics

GOLDEN SPORK Enemies deal double damage.
  1. Activate Cursed Mode Tier II.
  2. Open the portal to Mars and reach Round 60.
  3. Get a Mangler Cannon and travel to Mars. Shoot the portal with the Mangler Cannon, then teleport back to Machina Astralis.
  4. After a few seconds, the shot from the Mangler Cannon will leave the portal going towards Archive of Orbis. You need to gather a group of zombies inside the red circle on the ground near the portal, using Decoys for example.
  5. If enough zombies were killed, a red portal will appear on a wall at Crash Site. Interact with it to start the Relic Trial.
  6. Survive 6 waves while zombies and HVTs only take damage from hip-fire.
CIVIL PROTECTOR HEAD Perk decay — every 100 kills you lose a perk.
  1. Activate Cursed Mode Tier II.
  2. Reach Round 60 and make sure to have PhD Flopper.
  3. Get Energy Mine and trigger a mine next to Ol' Tessie. If done correctly, the headlights will flash three times signaling the start of a sequence. Afterwards each of the headlights and brake lights will light up. This will be the order for the chandeliers in Museum Infinitum.
  4. The two chandeliers near the doorway to Stargazer's Courtyard are the headlights, while the two chandeliers near the doorway to Crash Site are the brake lights.
  5. Wall jump to reach the chandeliers to put out the candles, in the order shown by Ol' Tessie.
  6. Travel to Crash Site and interact with the red portal to start the Relic Trial.
  7. Survive 6 waves by killing HVTs while all zombies are turned into sprinters. 🧮 organ order helper
