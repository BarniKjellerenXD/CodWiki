LGM-1

  1. Collect several items:
    1. Destroy one of O.S.C.A.R. flying drones to obtain the Damaged Drone.
    2. Find a blinking lamp post around the map and shoot it to drop the Aberrant Wiring.
    3. Equipped with a Pack-a-Punched gun, shoot the gearbox of Ol' Tessie in Crash Site to collect the Car Battery.
    4. With Cryofreeze equipped, find and shoot an Aether Crystal around the map to drop an Absolute Zero Shard. You need to collect a total of 3 shards.
  2. Kill O.S.C.A.R. using three different traps. This can be done in any order, but only one can be done per round.
    1. Place the Absolute Zero Shard inside the rocket thrusters in Museum Infinitum. If done correctly, a countdown will be heard. Activate the trap and bring O.S.C.A.R. to the rocket thrusters to kill it.
    2. Place the Damaged Drone inside the Electro-Volt Projector at the Luminarium. Bring O.S.C.A.R. inside the room and interact with the trap to kill it.
    3. Inside the Observatory Dome, find and shoot at 9 fuming valves around the room. If done correctly, the telescope will start moving.
      • Two are located above the telescope.
      • Two are located under the telescope.
      • Three are located on the walls inside the room.
      • One is located on a pillar behind a sparking box.
      • One is located in the small room when coming from Machina Astralis
    4. Bring O.S.C.A.R. beneath the telescope and interact with the organ in front of the Pack-a-Punch machine. A screen will appear and you'll need to place the cursor on the Sun to activate the trap. Make sure O.S.C.A.R. stays within the laser beam to kill it.
  1. Once all three traps are done, the final O.S.C.A.R. killed will drop the LGM-1.

Main Quest

Awaken the astronomer

  1. Acquire the LGM-1 (see Key Features).
  2. Starting at Round 10, follow O.S.C.A.R. until it calls out three planets. Convert planets to numbers by their distance from the Sun (Mercury = 1, Neptune = 8).
  3. Enter the 3-digit code at the Observatory Dome terminal; a lift drops with a corpse and a key.
  4. Go to Machina Astralis, open the cryopod with the key to reveal a corpse.
  5. Shoot the glass at Museum Infinitum to grab the Saw, return to the pod, and take the brain.
  6. Place the brain on the table in the Luminarium and defend during the short lockdown.
  7. Bring the brain to the machine near the teleporter in Machina Astralis to power it; it will list book titles.

Power and align the observatory

  1. Use the Observatory Dome telescope (requires trap activation or LGM-1) to find Mars coordinates (4-digit code).
  2. Enter the code at the Machina Astralis machine; it confirms Mars.
  3. In Archive of Orbis, match the three busts to the counted book titles from their shelves (do this at round start for time).
  4. When correct, climb the opened panel to grab the Neptune model and place it atop Machina Astralis.
  5. Collect three planet notes (directions + planet): Luminarium desk, Machina Astralis table, Archive of Orbis table.
  6. Align the planets on the model display to the noted directions (compass around the Sun model).
  7. Return to Machina Astralis, interact with the brain to reboot the portal, defend against O.S.C.A.R.; you will be sent to Mars.

Mars sequence

  1. Pick up the brain at the arena center, place it in the machine up the stairs, and create the portal.
  2. Each round, play the machine sound to spot the ascendant eye; while it flies, shoot pylons: Back Right > Back Left > Front Right > Front Left > temple pylon above the machine. Grab the eye when it dives.
  3. Place the ascendant eye in the device on the right side of the arena (facing where you shot pylons).
  4. With the LGM-1, shoot the antennas of the five arena pillars until each shows a symbol and a song plays.
  5. Back in Observatory Dome, read the organ screen: it shows the five symbols in order with one missing (denoted by wavy lines). Note the missing symbol and its position.
  6. Return to Mars and interact with the pillars in that exact order, inserting the missing symbol where indicated.
  7. Interact with the machine atop the stairs to start the fight against Caltheris.

Caltheris Boss Fight

Phase 1 - Laser and lava orbs

Kill zombies while dodging lasers to charge the right Tesla coil; fire the brain beam. Repeat on the left; lava orbs leave burning pools.

  • Gas flood + poison zombies: climb the stairs when the floor fills.
Phase 2 - Small golem

Chases, slaps, throws rocks. Damage until a blue weak point appears, then focus fire while kiting zombies.

Phase 3 - Larger golem

Same kit plus slam shockwave. Jump as the slam lands to avoid damage.

Phase 4 - Empowered golem

All attacks hit harder. Rock throw leaves purple electric pools; slam has bigger radius, longer stun—keep moving and jump slams.

Side Quests and Easter Eggs

Secret song

Find three Mr Peeks headphones around the map:

  • At the bottom shelf, left of the exit door in Luminarium
  • On a display case in Machina Astralis
  • On the ground next to a wall in Mars

By Avenged Sevenfold

Alpha Omega Game Over Song

Find three records:

  • Inside a wooden box in Machina Astralis in a box on the middle floor
  • At the top of a bookshelf in the Luminarium
  • On a wooden sil above a zombie spawner in the Museum

Bring the records to gramophone in the Luminarium and it will play the Game Over song from Alpha Omega.

Friendly Ravager

Look for glowing eyes in zombie spawners around the map and throw a shiny trinket at them; the Ravager will eat the trinket and later reappear in another zombie spawn. Repeat this process twice more, and on the third time he will jump out, clap his cymbals together, and become a temporary friendly Ravager.

Relics

There are several Relics that can be unlocked from playing Astra Malorum, those Relics are then used as gameplay modifiers for Cursed mode.

The Relics are divided into three groups: Grim, Sinister, and Wicked. Each group fill the Cursed Tier bar differently with the Grim Relics filling up a third of the bar, the Sinister Relics filling up two thirds, and the Wicked Relics filling up the entire bar. As such you will need to apply different Relics to reach the desired Tier level.

  • Tier I - Golden Armor available (45,000 Essence)
  • Tier II - Ultra Rarity available (8000 Salvage or 17500 Salvage from default rarity)
  • Tier III - Pack-a-Punch Level IV available (100,000 Essence)
