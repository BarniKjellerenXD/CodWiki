Totenreich Uranium Pincers Solver

Mark the tubes already in the wall, and the solver shows where the 3 uranium tubes must go.

How it works — the 4×4 grid shows the wall of tube sockets. Tap the 6 cells that already hold a tube (they turn gold). The solver then works out every way to place the 3 uranium tubes so the wall forms a single big chunk with the right leftovers, and lights those cells red.
① Grid — tap cells that already hold a tube tap again to undo
