# BO3 original-map guides and earlier-game catalogue

Scope: Shadows of Evil, The Giant, Der Eisendrache, Zetsubou No Shima, Gorod Krovi and Revelations. Zombies Chronicles stays separate. Research date: 3 October 2026.

## Options and decision

| Option | Benefit | Drawback | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expand the existing text and link out for pictures | Small implementation | Players leave the step to identify an object | Rejected |
| Add a checklist for every quest branch | Lots of saved state | Repeats Quick Parts without solving the player's problem | Rejected |
| Illustrated walkthroughs with visual matching, sequence memory and recipe/location lookup | The clue and next action stay together | More research and image validation | Selected |

Keep the established gold palette, GuideArticle, Quick Parts, contents, pins, image lightbox and shared saved state. This is an extension of the existing Read/Operate surfaces. Every map gets setup, equipment, complete main-quest instructions, failure recovery, side Easter eggs and sources. Optional bow branches and equipment have distinct anchors. Preserve existing phase/step IDs where their meaning remains the same.

## Tool decisions

- Shadows: select the actual train glyphs; retain older notes without pretending text is a recognized symbol. Put the four-player finale assignment instructions in the guide.
- Der Eisendrache: a photographed Void glyph mapper and a visual safe/terminal board. Bow requirements and all four upgrade routes stay in the guide; the redundant bow helper is retired after user feedback. Names do not have universal glyph mappings.
- Zetsubou: choose a plant goal to see why it is needed, required equipment and a three-round care schedule. A water dropdown shows photographed pool locations and the timed Rainbow pickup. Beginner help explains seeds, buckets, ordinary planters and the special Masamune planter; observations are logged separately from instructions. Random outcomes stay explicitly random.
- Gorod: photograph-backed valve inputs and verified routing; bomb recording in SOPHIA's displayed order, with correction and one-at-a-time defuse guidance. No fabricated live timer.
- Revelations: a region/object location finder and photographed rune-order recorder. Distinguish the four real creation runes from decoy symbols.
- The Giant: the short Fly Trap and hidden-perk paths belong in the guide, rather than a second checklist tool.

Retired checklist URLs redirect to their relevant guide anchors. Existing browser observations are retained under their original storage keys. Both inline and full-page helpers use the same versioned state, undo and reset behavior.

## Earlier games

After the BO3 guides, add separate BO2, BO1 and World at War categories. Each map has an explicit map-entry page marked “Guide planned”, rather than a pretend completed walkthrough. Include BO2 survival/Grief/Turned locations and BO1's World at War remasters with edition labels. Dead Ops Arcade is labelled a bonus mode. No new desktop keyboard shortcuts are assigned.

## Verification

Check rules and invalid input, legacy notes, generated page anchors, source links, local images, catalogue/search and desktop navigation. Run the existing suite and production build; inspect desktop and mobile and exercise the tools. Source/software validation does not constitute an in-game playthrough.
