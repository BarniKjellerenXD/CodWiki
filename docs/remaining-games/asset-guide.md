# Image selection and capture plan

The full [catalogue](assets.json) records exact image/source URLs, local paths, SHA-256, dimensions, creator credit, review status and rights status. The [map groups](asset-groups.json) associate those IDs with the thirty destinations. The [seasonal MWZ atlas](mw-aether-atlas.json) adds contract/exit/key/door identities, source grids and their local screenshots.

The collection contains uncropped reference originals. It is deliberately outside `public/`. Its approximately 511 MiB of images should not become a 511 MiB site download. Select only the views needed by the published step; preserve the research originals here or in a separate archive. Most images are file-validated references, with publication clearance and detailed caption review still separate.

## Production formatting

Prefer a wide landmark shot paired with a close object shot for hard-to-find items. Keep comparable screenshots at the same camera angle when showing a before/after state. Do not use decorative cover art as instructional evidence. GIF/video supplements should have a still poster and text explanation; information cannot depend on animation alone.

Store web variants in maintained production image paths with explicit intrinsic dimensions, responsive sizes and lazy loading below the fold. A glyph selector should preserve fine strokes and have an enlarged original available. Do not upscale a 640px reference and label it a high-resolution gameplay capture. Use text/coordinates alongside colour, a useful alt description, and a caption naming the edition, location and action.

For future interactive maps, the artwork, projection, floors, source edition and landmarks are reviewed together. The research grids and WZHUB marker IDs are sufficient for illustrated references; they are not verified inputs to CodWiki's own coordinate system.
