Reviewed 3 October 2026. Scope: the eight BO3 remasters, with setup, equipment, main quests where present, side quests, music and rewards. Existing BO3 guides and saved observations remain compatible.

## Approach selected

Considered: a checklist for every map; standalone route summaries; or illustrated guides with helpers only for visual identification, changing sequences and difficult searches. Selected the third approach. Fixed routes belong in the guide, while photographs, symbols and match observations earn a dedicated workspace.

- Origins: photographed ice-symbol matcher, fire-torch decoder, searchable part/record/upgrade location atlas. Fixed wind and lightning solutions stay beside their instructions.
- Shangri-La: visual matching-tile notebook with duplicate detection; eight photographed gongs with confirmed good/bad notes. The same gongs support the main quest and Samantha side quest.
- Moon: four-screen sequence recorder, correct left-to-right layout; laboratory panel finder for the timed Hacker step.
- Nacht, Verrückt and Kino: photographed Samantha search locations. Kino also retains its knock recorder and explains the three distinct rounds.
- Ascension: photographed Gersh capture targets and ordered Samantha targets.
- Shi No Numa: photographed pan and bridge instructions. Its perk notebook remains a small optional observation aid; no extra tracker.
