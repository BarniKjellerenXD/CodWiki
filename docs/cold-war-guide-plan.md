# Cold War guide expansion

Reviewed 2026-10-02. Scope: Die Maschine, Firebase Z, Mauer der Toten, Forsaken, and all eight Outbreak regions, with separate Ravenov Implications and Operation Excision walkthroughs. These are the existing Cold War story-map routes; Onslaught and Dead Ops Arcade are separate modes.

## Options and decision

| Approach | Strength | Cost | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expand prose and link to external images | Fast to maintain | Interrupts play and loses visual context | Rejected |
| Give every quest branch a tracker | More controls | Duplicates Quick Parts without helping solve anything | Rejected |
| Illustrated map guides plus contextual visual helpers | Photos explain actions; inputs answer a specific question | Requires source reconciliation and local assets | Selected |

Keep the established GuideArticle, Quick Parts, contents, pins, image lightbox, gold palette and shared saved-state behavior. Full Details contains equipment routes, main quests, side eggs, recovery advice and point-of-no-return warnings. Preserve existing phase and step IDs; new detail gets new IDs. Outbreak's two quest routes retain independent progress.

## Chosen tools

- **Firebase Z dartboard:** click the same sector seen on the computer; show the three real board numbers and the final bullseye. No counting sectors in a dropdown. Preserve the existing sector schema.
- **Mauer safe:** fixed room-to-dial order, all nine possible clue photographs, two-digit validation and leading zeros.
- **Outbreak locations:** quest, stage and region select the actual annotated map. Explain unavailable projector/red-rift combinations. Include Zoo masks and D.I.E. crates.
- **Outbreak launch:** three console-light observations produce the A/B/D order, detect contradictions and distinguish deductions. Preserve old acceptance/rejection notes in a secondary view.
- **Forsaken TVs:** photograph-backed color memory for 4/8/12 flashes, editable numbered slots and a manual shot-by-shot playback. No invented universal sequence.
- **D.I.E. variants:** replace the checkbox tracker with a visual chamber-port and upgrade reference; select a port to see its weapon, route and quest prerequisites.

Retire Mimic-memory and neutralizer checklists to their detailed guide sections. Retain compatibility URLs and saved browser keys.

## Research policy

Use Reddit hunt threads for discovery and corrections, corroborate the quest with independently written guides, and use mmmrkennedy's annotated gameplay photos for object sightlines. Original instructions are rewritten for this product. Asset provenance is recorded in `cold-war-assets.json` beside the research notes. Screenshots are game references, not generated art. Older guides can omit later additions or disagree on timers; the in-game HUD controls live countdowns.

Notable reconciliations: `/r/CODZombies/wiki/outbreak/` describes Advanced Warfare and is not a Cold War source. Mauer's three clue rooms have fixed dial positions. Klaus needs the first defended upgrade for the train; the extra floppy-disk upgrades are optional. Outbreak console lights reveal order. Firebase's quest Mimics are disguised-loot spawns, not arbitrary Mimics captured in a room. Forsaken's pizza reward is random. Late-game quests cannot be started in Outbreak's three-region playlist.
