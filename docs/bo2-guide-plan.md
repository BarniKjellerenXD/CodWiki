# Black Ops II map guides

Scope: the original BO2 editions of TranZit, Die Rise, Mob of the Dead, Buried and Origins; Nuketown Zombies; Bus Depot, Town and Farm Survival; Diner Turned; Cell Block Grief; Borough Grief / Turned. Research date: 3 October 2026.

## Approach and choices

| Approach | Benefit | Limitation | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Link to a single walkthrough | Fast reference | Incomplete side quests, conflicting claims and missing images | Reject |
| Tool for every phase | Many controls | Duplicates Quick Parts and slows players down | Reject |
| Cross-checked illustrated guides with focused puzzle helpers | Read the clue, understand it and act in the same place | Requires version and source checks | Select |

Extend the established charcoal-and-gold Read / Operate surfaces. Keep GuideArticle, image enlargement, contents, pins, Quick Parts and shared puzzle persistence. No new progress-tracker tools. Main quests state difficulty, player counts, prerequisites, success cues and recovery. Survival and competitive maps explain their actual modes and available secrets without inventing a story quest.

## Tool decisions

- Die Rise: photographed Mahjong reference with colour pairing and ordered tower strikes. Detect duplicate colours and incomplete observations; explain the north anchor.
- Buried: visually match the first two cipher glyphs to its mine sign; narrow the 24 maze-lever permutations using actual spark feedback; map the mansion's nine light positions to bells tested in the current match. Candy jar colours are not a solution. Bell positions require calibration rather than a guessed universal order.
- Origins: reuse verified Ice and Fire symbol photographs and matching UI with independent BO2 state. Keep fixed Wind and Lightning sequences as illustrated instructions.
- TranZit, Mob and the smaller maps: equipment, routes, fixed codes and tactical guidance belong in the guide. Do not add a checklist masquerading as a tool.

## Source handling

Use Reddit alongside illustrated community walkthroughs, Steam guides and Call of Duty Maps. Some Reddit pages contain unfinished sections, mismatched weapon tables or inconsistent target counts; do not import those as facts. Document useful discrepancies and credit image sources. BO2 Origins is authored separately from Zombies Chronicles; no GobbleGum or remaster-only doll secret.

## Verification

Delivered 12 map/mode guides, 82 sections and 240 steps, including 102 illustrated steps. The 180 new locally stored, credited images supplement the existing Origins symbol photographs. Six focused helpers are integrated with the corresponding quest steps, catalogue, search, desktop navigation and Quick Parts.

- The complete automated suite passes: 168 tests, zero failures. BO2 coverage checks guide/mode coverage, IDs, section links, image bytes and provenance, crop bounds, invalid observations, all 24 lever solutions, bell calibration, persistence normalization and independent BO2/BO3 Origins state.
- The production build passes. HTTP smoke checks pass for all 12 guide routes and six helper routes.
- Browser checks cover 1280 × 900 desktop and 390 × 844 phone layouts, photographed symbols, decoder results, saved state after reload, shared inline/full-page state, undo, reset confirmation and image enlargement. The mobile review led to a sticky bell callout, automatic focus on saved lever results and shorter Mahjong colour prompts; the final layouts were checked again.
- The final source review resolved the TranZit Maxis two-player route, Die Rise's bank and Sliquifier locations, Buried buildable parts and piano perk, and Borough's actual weapon/perk differences. Nuketown's disputed song trigger is qualified in the guide. Incomplete Reddit sections and uncertain persistent-upgrade counts were not treated as settled facts.

These are source and software checks, not an in-game playthrough. Images, original URLs and credits are recorded in `docs/bo2-assets.json`; each guide includes its research sources.
