## Provenance

The new SVGs are original CodWiki connection diagrams derived from the edition guides’ stated relationships. They do not copy or trace the fan images whose usage was unresolved in research. Every drawn edge has a source guide, exact step ID and evidence text in [classic-map-implementation.json](classic-map-implementation.json). Shape, distance and angle are illustrative; markers use area precision. Disconnected regions mean their intermediate route is not asserted.

The Origins image is by **u/ZedzDEMIZie**, used under the creator’s explicit permission to use it with credit in [the original post](https://www.reddit.com/r/CODZombies/comments/6wh7nx/hq_map_i_made_to_celebrate_originss_4th_birthday/). It was visually inspected at 5760 × 3240; the credit remains on the image. Baked icons remain visible when overlay filters are changed. BO2 and Chronicles get independent targets and descriptions.

Regenerate only these owned outputs with `node scripts/build-classic-maps.mjs`. The generator does not touch the catalogue, shared loader, guide files or viewer.
