## Images and helpers

`bo3-assets.json` records every downloaded gameplay image's original URL, guide URL, source credit and review date. Images are local WebP, JPEG and PNG files; no image-generation model fabricated glyphs or locations. Symbol controls use viewport crops of the original screenshot and retain a link to the credited full reference. Guide illustrations support the existing lightbox. The Revelations finder covers 16 egg sites, 12 buried rune sites, six bones and seven relics.

Eight helpers replace the earlier generic notes/checklists: train symbol matching; Void name/symbol matching; safe and terminal memory; plant recipes, water locations and round planning; valves; bomb order with photos and manual callouts; Kronorium scoreboard order; and the Revelations location finder. Four redundant helper URLs redirect to their guide sections. Their browser keys are not deleted. Existing text fields and checklist fields remain declared under the existing v1 schema so upgrading the UI does not erase them.

## Plant-tool feedback revision

The bow requirements/routes helper was retired after user feedback. Its URL redirects to the preserved `details-bows` guide section, which still links all four upgrades. Both Revelations tools remain active.

Zetsubou's helper is now **Plant recipes & water locations**. Each recipe explains its quest purpose, equipment and planter requirements. Beginner help uses actual seed and bucket inventory icons and a clearly captioned photo of a grown pod in an ordinary stone planter. The water dropdown has photographed Blue, Green and Purple pools; Rainbow includes both the sewer entrance and the timed rock pickup. The three care rounds show actions before the observation controls, and the planting round determines the harvest round. Existing v1 observations are preserved, including earlier KT-4 checks when comparing an untreated recipe.

Water directions and plant basics were reconciled against the Reddit wiki, the existing illustrated quest, the Steam plant reference, and the [CoDZ Library plant guide](https://www.callofdutyzombies.com/zombie-library/zombies-library/black-ops-iii/zetsubou-no-shima/seeds-waters-and-fruits-of-labor-r424/). Rainbow collection uses the lower-bunker-to-Lab-B sewer route, costs 500 points and works with an ordinary bucket; the timed pickup must be checked in the inventory. No fixed imprint probability is claimed. Six additional source images bring the asset manifest to 252 entries, retaining the actual JPEG/PNG formats rather than relabelling them WebP.

The revised suite has 151 passing tests, including bow-route retirement, illustrated water references and recipe-to-guide links. The production build succeeds. Browser verification covers preserved inputs, the first-use explanation, all four water references, the three-round schedule, desktop and mobile layouts, and the shared image lightbox. Follow-up screenshots use the `plants-` prefix in `.impeccable/review/`. These remain software and source checks, not an in-game playthrough.
