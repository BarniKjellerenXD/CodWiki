# BO6: Citadelle des Morts and The Tomb

Researched 2026-10-02. Authoring source: `shared/bo6-castle-tomb.mjs`; generated pages should be regenerated, not hand-edited. This is a researched synthesis, not a claim of a personally completed in-game verification run. Both public guides make that limitation visible and identify Standard mode for complete optional content.

## Coverage and route

Citadelle has 9 main phases / 29 main steps, covering castle access, Pack-a-Punch, Krafft, all four sword quests, all four incantations, bottle runes, pages and traps, knight salutes, orb trials and the Guardian. Its 7 side sections / 15 side steps cover bartender PhD, four Peeks, following the raven, rune-room lockdown, cheese and all ten rats, chess guardian, fireplace protector, cannon repair, bell tower, wishing-well deposit/withdrawal, Maya's character quest, both songs, pool table, all eight hidden power-ups and useful map services. Grouped steps list individual item positions and dependencies in their expanded explanations.

The Tomb has 5 main phases / 20 main steps, covering the excavation, both Staff constellation defenses, the Nexus Staff build, lantern/rock/gateway upgrade, escort, all four statue trials and the Sentinel Artifact. Its 7 side sections / 27 side steps cover both Golden Armor sacrifices, all four shovels, Ancient Gems/free Ray Gun, each of the ten Self-Revive vases with its own photograph, waterfall tool, floating-rock crystal, statue memory game, braziers, generators, fungal loot, X-marked skulls, jumpscare, both songs, all eight hidden power-ups and services.

Steps explain what opens the next action, where to stand/look, what confirms success and what to do after a failed attempt where sources establish a recovery. The main route is viable solo or cooperatively. Staff survival and bullet-weapon boss damage are distinguished explicitly. Bosses are described by their visible state changes rather than unreliable phase labels shared inconsistently by sources.

Decisions where sources differ:

- Raven horn uses **Aries + Fire**, confirmed against the community wiki, illustrated chart and other references. A conflicting early Taurus label was rejected. The actual wheel's inner ring has elemental triangles and outer ring has zodiac glyphs; sources reversing their prose labels were not followed.
- The Lion quest uses glowing golden Parasites to charge the blade. An illustrated guide's mistaken enemy label was reconciled against the community route and its screenshots.
- Book reading order is top left, bottom left, top right, bottom right. Trap photographs have sometimes misleading room filenames; filenames are not treated as location evidence. The tool asks users to save their own observed trap location.
- Rat plate room labels differ between sources. The route uses the illustrated Oubliette landmark and refers to the plate, reducing dependence on an inconsistent room heading.
- The chess-piece search includes Dungeon as well as the three commonly repeated spawn spots. Only one piece is required.
- Wishing-well behavior is tied to the Vermin emerging and later eligible special rounds. Unverified older claims about a fixed four-grenade requirement are not used.
- Raven-follow timing is approximate (about three to four minutes), because sources report three minutes, three-and-a-half minutes or laps. The guide uses the actual item drop as confirmation.
- Tomb statue trial order is fixed Vermin → Parasite → Doppelghast → Amalgam. A completed defense and successful charge delivery are different milestones; losing a charge does not mean repeating a successful defense.
- The Sentinel fight is grouped into different phase counts by different publications. The guide follows purple damage windows, orange danger states, artifact carriers and the final back-wall damage check instead of promising a brittle count.
- Tomb's third northern vase is variously grouped into Dig Site or Roman Mausoleum. Its shared landmark, above the blue Nexus gateway, and individual screenshot identify the same object.
- Old Golden Armor pre-activation scorestreak exploits are excluded. The guide explains the intended one-health fights and cancellation by leaving the ritual area.

## Tool alternatives and selected behavior

1. Text-only Raven lookup is compact but assumes a player can name an unfamiliar fossil and recognize astrological symbols. Selected a photographed antiquity selector and paired photographed ring symbols, with explicit bottom-pointer alignment. No made-up turn count is produced.
2. A fixed bottle-answer chart is incorrect across matches. A free-text scratchpad still asks users to invent names for runes. Selected a 20-glyph photographic picker that records six numbered observations in order, plus a second mode for the four actual book eye designs. Each selection advances the active slot; any slot remains editable. Accepted wall shots and dimmed book pages are user-confirmed, not simulated game validation.
3. A stylized Tomb symbol font risks shape errors. A whole reference image is hard to use on a narrow screen. Selected eight exact screenshot crops; choosing the three marked rocks highlights their matching cells on a two-column gateway. The tool requires exactly three observations and never supplies a default answer. Roman numerals and the source map remain available as secondary references.
4. Generic checklists are retained only for tasks that benefit from memory across travel: knight/orb progression, Tomb defense-versus-return milestones and ten vase locations. They do not pretend to calculate randomized puzzle answers.

Custom IDs: `bo6-citadelle-raven`, `bo6-citadelle-symbols`, `bo6-tomb-symbols`. All are handled by `CastleTomb.vue`; `CastleTombGlyph.vue` crops existing images with unique, explicit SVG clip paths, so letterboxing cannot expose adjacent reference symbols. Shared `usePuzzleState` provides persistence and undo; reset requires confirmation. Helper mode/slot selection and observed values survive a reload and are shared between inline and full-page tools. Visual state also has text labels; controls meet the incumbent 44px target and focus treatment. No new design system or glyph artwork was introduced.
